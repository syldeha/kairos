# Kairos's long-term memory for the AMI remote-control design meetings (one line = one memory).
# Based on the project brief given to the AMI scenario teams.
The team is designing a new television remote control for the company Real Reaction.
The remote control should sell for 25 euros.
The production cost must stay at or below 12.50 euros per unit.
The profit target for the project is 50 million euros.
The remote is for television only; teletext is considered outdated and should not be included.
The company's corporate colour and slogan must be recognisable in the design.
The product is aimed at the international market.
